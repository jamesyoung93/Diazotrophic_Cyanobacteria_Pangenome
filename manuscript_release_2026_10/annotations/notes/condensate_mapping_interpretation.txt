CONDENSATE MAPPING SENSITIVITY — author-review analysis, not a source-data rewrite

The independent reconstruction matches all 1,311 released family best scores,
ranks and mapped-row counts. It also reproduces the v2 condensate component and
tier for all1,457 candidate rows before applying any sensitivity exclusions.
Every source file and the current167-family scoring artifact remains unchanged.

1. Accession ambiguity
The source protein accession map has55 accessions with multiple retained GF
memberships. Only5 selected condensate mappings use those ambiguous accessions.
Excluding those5 rows makes five family annotations missing. Only one scored
condensate component changes: GF_00615 (F0F1 ATP synthase gamma),0.4→0.0. It remains
excluded as housekeeping. All167 shortlisted families and all22 TierA remain,
with65 active-phase-up annotations. This result does not resolve the underlying
accession ambiguity; it bounds its effect on this prioritization rule.

2. Homolog-cluster fallback
804/2,125 mapped protein rows use cluster_id because the direct protein accession
does not map. Removing these804 rows leaves1,321 direct rows covering ALL1,311
families. Thus fallback adds no family coverage in this dataset, but it changes
the released best score/rank for164 families (218 families have at least one
fallback row). This distinction matters when describing the overlay: some best
scores come from the homolog-cluster transfer, not the directly mapped protein.

Two prespecified analyses keep the original1,311-family denominator/reference:
- Conservative missing annotation: if the retained direct rows no longer supply
  the released best score/rank, disable that family's condensate annotation and
  set its component to0. Unaffected family ranks remain fixed.36 candidate
  numeric scores change.
- Fixed-reference placement: for a changed best value, position the best retained
  direct score (then cyano rank) against the ORIGINAL family reference, excluding
  its own original entry, with denominator1,311. This is a sensitivity placement,
  not a reranking of all surviving families.22 candidate numeric scores change.

Both analyses produce the same sole tier migration: GF_02157 (acetolactate
synthase small subunit) falls from TierB to TierE. Its released percentile is
76/1,311 (5.80%) and composite score4.55. The direct-only best score places at
145/1,311 (11.06%), lowering the condensate component0.30→0.20 and final score
4.55→4.45, just below4.5. Disabling the annotation entirely gives4.25.
The sensitivity shortlist is166 =22A+139B+5HP, with64 active-up annotations.
All22 TierA memberships and condensate best annotations remain unchanged.

3. Combined exclusion
Removing both types excludes809 rows. The same sole GF_02157 tier migration
occurs. No additional family leaves the shortlist. Selected direct mappings
retain the source direct-first policy, including the22 cases where the mapped
cluster ID points to another family. These conflicts are disclosed separately;
they are not automatically evidence that the direct mapping is wrong.

Interpretation for manuscript/author decision
The22-family TierA band is insensitive to these two mapping choices; the broader
shortlist has one borderline family whose TierB placement depends on fallback
scoring. This is robustness of a heuristic threshold, not biological validation,
equivalence of mapping strategies, or a demonstrated causal role of condensates.
Keep167 as the explicitly defined v2 rule if retaining the historical mapping,
and report166 in the direct-only sensitivity. Alternatively choose a direct-only
rule explicitly and update dependent counts; do not silently replace the167
artifact or recompute all percentiles using a smaller denominator. Full-inventory
diagnostics should identify which mapping/percentile convention they use.
