# What this package can and cannot establish

The replay verifies arithmetic, joins, eligibility rules, strata and model-output summaries from the supplied inputs. It does not independently recover missing sequence evidence, validate mechanisms or replace experimental tests.

**Missing cross-atlas sequence mapping.** The related449-family resource lacks a recoverable family-membership/protein sequence crosswalk to this2286-family pangenome. Matching `GF_` identifiers in separately generated namespaces is not a valid family join. Equal normalized product strings are descriptive annotation agreement, not proof of homologous-family identity. Consequently the proposed score disables the identifier-based atlas component and imported HGT penalty. The source cache remains only to reproduce and document the original error. No product-name match is substituted as a new score.

**Protein mapping.** Current accession-to-family membership is supplied and auditable. Fifty-five protein accessions have two retained family memberships; the released condensate join picks the first. Only five selected condensate rows use these ambiguous accessions. Direct and cluster-ID mappings can also point to different families; the source gives direct mappings priority. The direct-only sensitivity evaluates804 homolog-cluster fallback rows separately. It does not establish sequence identity for external historical accession bridges. See the row-level mapping outputs and the interpretation note.

**Underlying clustering and functional identity.** The matrix contains 2,286 families, but the candidate inventory provides populated product annotations for 1,457. Current accession-to-family memberships are included. Protein sequences, CDS coordinates, seed alignments and full proteome FASTAs sufficient to independently validate the 80%-identity cluster boundaries or a cross-atlas orthology mapping are not included. A cluster's absence does not establish absence of a named protein function; common product labels can occur in multiple clusters. Do not infer paralogy or orthology from labels alone.

**Scoring and model evaluation.** The frozen 476 inventory includes473 estimable positive univariate carrier/noncarrier contrasts and three ubiquitous edge cases. The proposed tier rule excludes the latter without rewriting the released inventory or numeric score history. The score's model term is a flat historical inventory weight. Released importance ordering and corrected cross-validation importance are separate quantities. All 22 current Tier A families are eligible in all 50 repeated LR fits, but18 show both conditional coefficient signs. This supports reporting sensitivity, not asserting stable mechanistic direction.

This annotation bundle consumes saved model fits. Full prediction-model refitting, fold construction, train-only filtering and label-status sensitivity belong in the separate model audit. A successful annotation replay does not itself reproduce model AUCs.

**Statistical interpretation.** Family prevalence, study detection opportunities and annotation availability are intertwined. Adjusted regressions and coarse strata describe the supplied data; wide intervals and limited overlapping prevalence support constrain strong comparisons. Product-pattern composition and observational protein abundance are not causal validations. The original historical diagnostics are retained for traceability, with qualified labels where the original prose overstated what a calculation establishes.

**Environment.** Relative paths, fresh extraction and a separate clean scientific environment are tested on Windows. Package inputs include normalized measurements, not every original publisher supplement or every upstream processing intermediate. The initial module/distribution-version mismatches are disclosed in `runtime_versions.json`; the clean-environment proof records matching pinned versions and numerically identical results. Other operating systems were not tested. No input hashes are silently replaced when a source changes: update the scientific provenance and independently reviewed expectations before treating a new run as equivalent.
