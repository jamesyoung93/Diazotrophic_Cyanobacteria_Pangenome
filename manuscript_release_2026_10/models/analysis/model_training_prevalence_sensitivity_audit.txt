ADDITIONAL TRAINING-PREVALENCE SENSITIVITY (20 FITS; NOT PRIMARY REPLACEMENT)

The primary corrected evaluation holds the released family vocabulary fixed.
Here we additionally require at least 40 training carriers in each original
seed-42 fold, together with the same exact training-only purity boundaries and
minimum-three-training-genus filter. All 426 genomes, labels, folds, models and
hyperparameters remain unchanged. No retuning or candidate reranking occurs.

This nested eligibility rule is identifiable from the supplied 2286-family matrix:
a fixed family present in fewer than 40 genomes globally cannot reach 40 carriers
in a training subset. It does not reconstruct the global clustering/family definitions.
An absolute threshold of 40 also becomes a stricter relative-prevalence requirement
in smaller training cohorts (about 10.1–14.2%, versus 9.39% of the full426).
It is therefore an altered-eligibility sensitivity, not an estimate that isolates
the causal bias of unsupervised global selection or a replacement primary analysis.

All four classifiers use their original settings and one CPU/BLAS thread. LR
scaling remains training-only. The exact saved masks are nested inside the primary
training masks; no family is newly added. Full metrics and OOF probabilities are
saved separately and no original outputs or frozen inventories are overwritten.
