SAMPLE IDENTITY AND GROUPING SENSITIVITY -- AUDIT ONLY

Two negative-labeled records, GCF_047302775.1 (stored Synechococcus sp. MIT
S9506) and GCF_047302855.1 (stored Prochlorococcus marinus SS51), have identical
178-family binary vectors, identical179 retained protein-accession/family pairs,
and matching saved assembly length1,627,281bp, GC31%, one-contig/N50 metadata
and nif-hit fields. Their original held-out folds are4 and2 respectively.
These observations concern retained features/accessions and summary metadata;
they do not demonstrate identity of the complete genomic sequences.

The public NCBI Datasets snapshots retrieved2026-09-30 UTC show MIT S9506
currently suppressed with a contamination reason, whereas SS51 is current.
Their public assembly summary statistics also match, including GCcount507552,
and BioSamples differ. Current status is not backdated to original collection.
Thus remove_MIT_S9506 is a currently-suppressed-record sensitivity; remove_SS51
is the paired influence check, not a recommendation that its record is invalid.
Public evidence: sample_identity_public/GCF_047302775.1.json,
GCF_047302855.1.json, cohort_batch_*.json and retrieval_manifest.json.

Design
The two single-row exclusions preserve the original seed42 fold IDs of every
surviving row. All four classifiers and two one-predictor Logistic Regression
baselines are refit with original model defaults and exact training-only purity
(10*k>n and10*k<9*n) and at least3 training genera. Standardization is training
only. The fixed original2286-family matrix remains globally defined.

A separate conservative connected-group analysis links the stored genus labels
Synechococcus, Prochlorococcus and [Synechococcus]; Leptolyngbya and
[Leptolyngbya]; and Phormidium and [Phormidium]. It creates71 grouping units,
repartitions them using the original deterministic shuffled-round-robin splitter
with seed42, and uses these units for the training breadth filter. Bracketed
names may encode taxonomic uncertainty; this is a grouping sensitivity, not
a curated taxonomy correction. Merging entire genera is deliberately broader
than resolving the flagged pair. Grouping changes many train/test assignments.
The audit-group breadth filter changes zero selected features relative to
stored-genus breadth within those same connected-group folds.

Metrics
Unweighted AUC is averaged equally across five held-out folds, with sample SD.
Pooled genome AUC combines OOF scores giving each genome equal weight.
Genus-weighted AUC uses weight1/n_g per genome, recomputed in the evaluated
population. Stored-genus weights are retained for comparison; an additional
audit-group-weighted metric uses the71 connected groups. These are distinct
estimands, especially with unequal folds and differing fitted probability scales.
None is selected after observing which gives the strongest result.

Interpretation
Local removal has model-dependent influence. LR changes little for either row;
RF mean-fold AUC rises by approximately0.008–0.009, while XGBoost decreases
by approximately0.021–0.028. This is measurable influence and does not establish
that this pair alone drives discrimination. The same-row original-OOF reference
separates score changes from simply deleting a scored observation.

The connected-group mean-fold AUCs are0.8203 RF,0.8305 GB,0.8520 LR and
0.8439 XGBoost; length-only is0.7357 and family-count-only0.7489. One test
fold contains239 of426 genomes. This repartition cannot attribute its changes
specifically to the suspicious pair, alias handling or any single taxonomic
error. It tests a deliberately different blocking assumption. It is descriptive
single-partition sensitivity, not an inferential test or independent validation.
