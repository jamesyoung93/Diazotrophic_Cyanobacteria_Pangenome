Metric definitions and manuscript interpretation

Mean fold ROC-AUC gives each of the five held-out-genus folds equal weight and then
averages the within-fold genome-weighted AUCs. The accompanying SD describes their
spread; it is not a confidence interval. The folds contain 15 genera each but have
29-144 genomes and different class proportions, so this average does not give each
genome or each genus equal influence.

Pooled genome-weighted OOF AUC combines the 426 out-of-fold probabilities and gives
each genome equal weight. It also compares positives and negatives belonging to
different test folds, whose scores come from separately fitted models. It therefore
differs from mean fold AUC; the pooling should be explicitly labeled descriptive.

Equal-total-genus-weighted pooled OOF AUC uses weight 1/n_g for each genome in genus
g, where n_g is that genus's number of genomes in the evaluation panel. Every genus
has total weight one. Weighted AUC is the probability that a weighted positive has
a larger score than a weighted negative, counting ties as one half. The positive
and negative weights are normalized separately in that calculation. A mixed-label
genus contributes its positive fraction to positive weight and its negative fraction
to negative weight; this is not exactly equal weight per class-contributing genus.

Coverage: 75 genera contribute 112 positive and
314 negative genomes. Positive genomes occur in 44 genera and
negative genomes in 42 genera; 33 genera are positive-only,
31 negative-only and 11 mixed. Equal-total-genus weighting yields
positive class weight 37.677 and negative class weight 37.323, with
Kish effective class-contributing genus counts 39.59 and
39.24, respectively. These effective counts are (sum a_g)^2/sum a_g^2,
where a_g is the genus's total class weight; they describe weight concentration,
not independent sample sizes, degrees of freedom or inferential confidence bounds.
The corresponding genome-weighted genus concentration equivalents are
12.11 positive and 6.49 negative genera.
Positive class prevalence is 112/426 = 26.29% with genome weights and
50.24% with equal total genus weights. Average precision changes with this
weight-dependent prevalence; compare AP between models under the same weighting,
not directly across the genome-weighted and genus-weighted tasks.

Suggested compact Results wording:
"Mean fold ROC-AUC was 0.853-0.893 across the four classifiers (fold SD 0.078-0.181).
Descriptive pooled OOF AUC was 0.856-0.921 with equal genome weights and 0.782-0.815
when each genus had equal total weight. The latter comparison reduced the influence
of genera represented by many genomes; positive and negative genomes occurred in
44 and 42 genera, respectively. These summaries estimate different weighted
prediction tasks, and none replaces independent external validation."

Report all three definitions together if pooled results are shown. Do not select
the largest AUC. Family-content and size-only models below use identical splits,
metrics, runtime and default logistic-regression hyperparameters, without retuning.
