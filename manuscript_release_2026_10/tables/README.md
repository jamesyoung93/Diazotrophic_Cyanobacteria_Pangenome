# Supporting manuscript tables

These tables support comparisons and source attribution retained in the current manuscript.

- `related_atlas/`: product-label comparison with the related atlas, its split-overlap audit, and the 12-family nif-keyword audit. Product-label agreement does not establish cross-atlas family identity. The related model's matrix and predictions were unavailable for independent replay.
- `proteomics_sources/`: source-study index and mapping coverage. Current family annotations are reconstructed from normalized measurements in `../annotations/inputs/raw/normalized_proteins.csv`.

The files retain their source values. Their hashes and source commit locations are recorded in [supporting-table provenance](../validation/SUPPORTING_TABLES_SOURCE.json). Current prioritization is defined by the annotation results, not by a related-atlas score or a source-study coverage count.
